## What it does not say

QVault does not detect exploitation. Of the recorded in-the-wild 0-days, only those involving persistence or boot artefacts fall near this boundary, and for those the layer bounds blast radius and preserves evidence rather than preventing the 0-day. The remainder is out of scope and is not counted as coverage.

- Entries whose header mentions persistence or boot artefacts: **2 of 70**
- `browser_renderer`: 6
- `kernel`: 13
- `persistence`: 2
- `privilege_escalation`: 3
- `sandbox_escape`: 1
- `firmware_boot`: none. This corpus records exploited software 0-days; firmware implants such as those modelled elsewhere in this repository are not represented here, so its absence is not evidence they do not occur.

This corpus is exploitation research, not detection ground truth. It is not used to tune any rule, and a low overlap with it is expected rather than a criticism of either project.
