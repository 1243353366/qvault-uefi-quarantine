Published-source fingerprints and their defensive inversion

Generated from qvault/fingerprints.py by tools/gen_fingerprints.py. Do not edit by hand.

What this is not

No LoJax, MoonBounce or BlackLotus sample was handled to produce this catalogue. Nothing in this repository contains malware, and no entry below is primary-sourced. Each one comes from published analysis, carries that source, and is graded accordingly. assert_catalogue_integrity() refuses a CONFIRMED grade here, because a claim of primary analysis would be false.

Why a string match is only a lead

The same bytes appear in a compromised firmware image, in an EDR rule, and in the notes of anyone reading both. A textual match therefore caps at INFERRED, and every entry carries the benign explanation that most often produces it. LOJAX-1 is the clearest case: rpcnetp is also the legitimate Absolute/Computrace anti-theft agent, so its presence is normal on a great many OEM machines. Alerting on it without corroboration is the textbook false positive.

Detection discipline

  • A lead is an Observation, never a Finding. fingerprints.observations() returns observations, and a human supplies the benign-explanation adjudication that promotes one.
  • Hits never claim identification. Family attribution requires the two-independent-upstream rule from limitations.intel_trust.
  • Firmware claims need out-of-band evidence: an unnamed section found by an OS-mediated read is a lead about a possibly-forged blob, not firmware integrity (see firmware_integrity).
