## How a source is held

A bibliography entry is not decoration. Where a source makes a factual claim, the claim appears in `report/CLAIMS_LEDGER.csv` with that bibliography number, a locator, and a falsifier describing what observation would break it. Gate G3 fails if a claim has no source.
